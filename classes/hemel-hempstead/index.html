Thursday afternoons · Hemel Hempstead

Gentle Yoga in Hemel Hempstead

A small, friendly weekly gentle Hatha Yoga class in central Hemel Hempstead at Carey Baptist Church on Marlowes. Thursdays from 2:00pm to 3:15pm, taught by BWY-qualified teacher Penny Cronyn since 2004. £10 per session, booked as a block.

Spaces available now. This class is taking new students this term. Book a £10 trial session or call 07958 616470.

The class

Each session lasts 75 minutes and combines simple breathwork, mindful movement and gentle Hatha Yoga postures, finishing with a guided relaxation. The pace is unhurried and modifications are offered throughout, so the class suits beginners, returners, and people who want a kinder, less strenuous practice. Many regulars have been coming for years; new students are warmly welcomed.

Because it runs in the afternoon, the Thursday group is a mix of retired people, part-time and home workers, and anyone who prefers daytime to evening classes. It is the most central of Penny's venues and the easiest to reach by bus. If Thursday does not suit, see the other daytime classes in Hemel Hempstead.

Carey Baptist Church

Carey Baptist Church sits on Marlowes in central Hemel Hempstead, a short walk from the bus station. The room used for yoga is warm and quiet, and Penny keeps each class a comfortable size — never crammed.

Getting there

  • By bus: Hemel Hempstead bus station is a few minutes' walk away; many town routes also stop along Marlowes itself. Check Intalink for current timetables.
  • By car: Carey Baptist has a small car park at the front, subject to availability (see the Carey Baptist Church site). The Marlowes Car Park is a short walk away (PayByPhone, charged until 6pm).
  • By train: from Hemel Hempstead station, regular buses run every 15 minutes (about 8 minutes' journey), or it's roughly a 20-minute walk through Heath Park.

What to bring

  • A yoga mat.
  • Loose, comfortable clothing you can move in.
  • A blanket if you have one — useful for the relaxation at the end.

Pricing and booking

Classes are £10 per session and are booked as a block (typically one term at a time). If you are new to the class, Penny offers a one-off trial session so you can try a class before committing. Get in touch by email or phone to arrange a trial.

Common questions about the Thursday class

Is there space in the Thursday class?

Yes. The Thursday class has spaces for new students this term. Email or call Penny and she will confirm a date for your trial session.

Can I join part-way through a term?

Yes. Start with a trial session, then pay for the remaining weeks of the block.

I am in my 70s and have never done yoga. Is this the right class?

Very likely. The group leans towards a mature membership, the pace is slow, and chair-supported options are offered. If you are managing a health condition, speak to your GP or physiotherapist first and tell Penny before the class.
