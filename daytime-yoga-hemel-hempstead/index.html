Daytime Yoga Classes in Hemel Hempstead

Three small, friendly gentle Hatha Yoga classes run in the daytime in Hemel Hempstead each week: two on Tuesday at Leverstock Green Village Hall and one on Thursday at Carey Baptist Church in the town centre. They suit people who are retired, work part-time or from home, or simply prefer an afternoon class to an evening one. Taught by BWY-qualified teacher Penny Cronyn since 2004.

Spaces available now: the Thursday 2:00pm class at Carey Baptist Church is taking new students this term. Book a £10 trial session.

Thursday afternoons at Carey Baptist Church

The Thursday class meets in a warm, quiet room at Carey Baptist Church on Marlowes, a few minutes' walk from Hemel Hempstead bus station and the Marlowes Car Park. It is the easiest of the daytime classes to reach by public transport, and the one most likely to have room for a newcomer. Each session is 75 minutes of breathwork, mindful movement and gentle postures, ending with a guided relaxation. The Thursday class page has directions and parking details.

Tuesdays at Leverstock Green

The two Tuesday classes meet at Leverstock Green Village Hall, on the green in the village just east of Hemel Hempstead, with free parking on site. Both groups have been running for years and places are limited. If Tuesday is the only day that works for you, do ask: places come up between terms. See the Tuesday class page.

Who comes to a daytime class

The daytime groups lean towards a mature membership: people in their 60s, 70s and 80s, alongside shift workers, parents with children at school, and people working from home who want a proper break in the middle of the day. Nobody is asked to keep up with anyone else. Modifications are offered for every movement, and chair-supported options are available. If you are new to yoga, the page on gentle yoga for the over-60s describes what a class feels like from the inside.

If you are managing a health condition or returning after an operation, please speak to your GP or physiotherapist first about whether a group class is right for you at the moment, and tell Penny before the class so she can suggest modifications.

Common questions

Which daytime class has spaces at the moment?

The Thursday 2:00pm class at Carey Baptist Church has spaces for new students this term. Places in the Tuesday classes are limited; ask Penny whether there is a place or a waiting list.

Do I have to commit to a whole term?

Classes are booked as a block, usually a term at a time, but new students can try a single session first for the normal class price. If the class suits you, Penny will tell you how to join the block from the following week.

What should I bring?

A yoga mat, loose comfortable clothing, and a blanket for the relaxation at the end. Nothing else is needed.
